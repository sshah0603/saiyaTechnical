## Part 3: responders versus non-responders

Melanoma, miraclib, PBMC only. Each subject's repeat samples are averaged before testing, so a subject counts once.

**Finding.** No population separates responders from non-responders once the five tests are corrected for. n = 331 responder subjects, 325 non-responder.

Primary test is a two sided Mann-Whitney U. `p_value_adj` applies a Benjamini-Hochberg correction across the five populations and is the figure to quote. The Welch t-test is a cross-check. Cliff's delta reports how far the distributions are shifted, so a small p-value paired with a negligible delta can be flagged as thin.

## Caveats

- The five populations sum to 100 percent by construction, so one rising forces others down. A shift in monocytes and a shift in CD4 T cells are not independent findings.
- This is an observational comparison inside a treated arm, so a separating population is a candidate biomarker rather than a mechanism.
- Confirming either point needs a prespecified analysis on a held-out cohort.
